Welcome to Made for u

A community where fashion lovers help each other get dressed.

Whether you thrifted something new, need help for an occasion, or just love building outfits — Made for u connects you with people who love fashion as much as you do.

How to get started
1
Build your closet
Upload your pieces from Profile — tops, jackets, dresses, shoes. This is what stylists will work with.
2
Post a styling request
Tell the community what you need — a date night look, something for PFW, or how to style a new piece. Pin specific items you want styled.
3
Get styled — or style others
Browse Discover to see requests from the community. Pick pieces from their closet, add a note, and post your outfit. Rate the looks you love.

Style isn't about having all the answers — it's about discovering new possibilities together.
